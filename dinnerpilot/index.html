What DinnerPilot does

One app, one job.

DinnerPilot fits a week of meals to your grocery budget.

A week of meals on one screen, priced before you shop

Answer a few questions about your family once. DinnerPilot plans breakfast, lunch and dinner from 2,532 recipes, prices the week in estimated totals, and swaps any meal you don’t like in one tap.

See the features

Budget, diets and traditions, built in. The plan works around them.

Prices the plan up front

Set a weekly grocery budget, and every plan is priced against it in estimated totals.

Reminds you at 6:30

Set when you eat, and it alerts you when to start cooking.

Filters the plan for diets

Dietary needs and disliked ingredients filter every recipe before it reaches the plan.

Plans around your traditions

Switch on Taco Tuesday or Pizza Friday, and the plan fills those nights to match.

Pantry Chef turns your fridge into dinner

Type what you have, or photograph the shelf. Pantry Chef suggests three dinners in 5 to 10 seconds, sized to your family.

Part of DinnerPilot Pro. Every recipe can drop straight into your week.

Questions

Email DinnerPilot support

When can I get DinnerPilot?

It is in TestFlight for iPhone now; the App Store launch comes next. There is no Android version.

What is the price of Pro?

$5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, after a 7‑day free trial. Cancel anytime in your iPhone’s Settings.

Can I add my own recipes to the plan?

Yes. The free plan saves 5 custom recipes; Pro saves as many as you like.

Are the grocery prices real?

They are estimates from national averages, labeled that way in the app.

What about my family’s privacy?

Your data is never sold. The privacy policy lists exactly what the app stores.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete Account. The data deletion page has the other way.
