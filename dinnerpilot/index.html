Questions

Email DinnerPilot support

When can I get DinnerPilot?

It is in TestFlight for iPhone now; the App Store launch comes next. There is no Android version.

What is the price of Pro?

$5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, after a 7‑day free trial. Cancel anytime in your iPhone’s Settings.

Can I add my own recipes to the plan?

Yes. The free plan saves 5 custom recipes; Pro saves as many as you like.

Are the grocery prices real?

They are estimates from national averages, labeled that way in the app.

What about my family’s privacy?

Your data is never sold. The privacy policy lists exactly what the app stores.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete Account. The data deletion page has the other way.
