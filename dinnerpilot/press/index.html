York dad launches DinnerPilot, an iPhone app that plans a week of family dinners around a grocery budget

YORK, Maine, November 2026. Dave Murray, a York resident and father, has launched DinnerPilot, a free iPhone app that answers the nightly "what's for dinner?" question by planning a full week of family meals to fit a weekly grocery budget, then building the shopping list.

Most meal-planning apps start with recipes. DinnerPilot starts with a number. A family sets what they want to spend on groceries for the week, and the app plans breakfasts, lunches and dinners that fit it, with an estimated cost for each meal and for the week. It then builds one grocery list, grouped by store section, with pantry staples already checked off.

"Every night around five o'clock our house had the same conversation: what are we making, and do we even have it?" said Murray. "I wanted one place that plans the week, keeps us on budget, and hands me the shopping list. DinnerPilot is the app I wanted for my own family."

Families can swap any meal or ingredient with one tap, set dietary needs and foods to avoid, and keep traditions like Taco Tuesday or Friday pizza night in the plan. Cooking reminders are timed back from when the family wants to eat.

DinnerPilot is free on the App Store, with ads. DinnerPilot Pro removes ads and adds extra planning tools for $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a free first week. The app is made by Clubhouse Apps, Murray's small app studio based in York.

DinnerPilot is available now for iPhone in the United States: apps.apple.com/us/app/dinnerpilot/id6792765639

Fact sheet

  • App: DinnerPilot, for iPhone (US App Store)
  • Launched: November 2026
  • Made by: Clubhouse Apps, York, Maine
  • Price: Free with ads. DinnerPilot Pro $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, free first week
  • What it does: plans a week of breakfasts, lunches and dinners to a weekly grocery budget; estimated cost per meal and per week; one grocery list grouped by store section; one-tap meal and ingredient swaps; dietary needs and foods to avoid; family traditions; cooking reminders
  • Recipes: more than 2,500, each with step-by-step instructions. The catalog was drafted with the help of AI. Pro's Pantry Chef suggests dinner ideas from what you already have, also using AI. Prices are estimates and dietary filters are a guide, not a guarantee.

About the founder

Dave Murray lives in York, Maine, with his family of four. He built DinnerPilot to solve his own family's weeknight dinner scramble and started Clubhouse Apps to publish it. DinnerPilot is the studio's first app.
