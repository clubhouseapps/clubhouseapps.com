Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clubhouse Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DinnerPilot iOS application and the ClubhouseApps.com website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using DinnerPilot or this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your name (if you choose to share it).

Family profile: To personalize your meal plans, we collect information you provide including family size (number of adults and children), dietary restrictions, disliked foods, cooking preferences, cuisine preferences, maximum cook time, and weekly grocery budget.

Meal and usage data: We store your saved meal plans, grocery lists, meal history, liked and disliked recipes, and in-app activity related to meal planning features. Ingredient swaps you choose, including ones you type in, are stored on your device with your meal plan.

Your own recipes: Recipes you add are saved to your profile in the app. They are never shown to other users.

Ratings and comments (Pro): Pro members can rate recipes and post comments. Other people see a comment with only your first name (or "A DinnerPilot cook" if we have no name). We also keep, privately, the account that wrote each comment (its email address and account ID), the text of the comment, and what we decided about it, so we can review reports and act on repeated violations. Comment text is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for an automatic check before it is shown. Anyone with an account can report a comment or hide an author in the app; we keep those reports and who made them. Star ratings are stored with your account.

AI features (Pro): When you use Pantry Chef, the ingredients you type, the photos you take or select from your library, and the meal type, family size, dietary restrictions, foods you avoid, cuisine preference and budget level for that request are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to identify ingredients and generate recipes. When you use recipe photo scan, the photos of the recipe you choose are sent to Anthropic to read the recipe. We do not keep these photos on our servers. Anthropic processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, which describe how long it retains data. The first time you use one of these features, the app asks for your permission to send this information to Anthropic. You can turn that permission off at any time in the Profile tab. We keep a count of the AI requests your account makes each day so we can apply the daily and monthly limits.

Subscription status: If you subscribe to DinnerPilot Pro, our server receives Apple's signed transaction record to verify the purchase and stores with your account your subscription tier (Free or Pro), whether you are in a free trial, Apple's identifier for the subscription, and the date the current paid period ends. We never receive your payment card details.

Device and usage information: DinnerPilot does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting tools. For free-tier users, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad-interaction and diagnostic data, as described in Section 3. We do not collect precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized weekly meal plans tailored to your family
  • To create and sync your grocery lists across devices
  • To power AI features: recipe generation, ingredient identification from photos, and reading a recipe from a photo
  • To remember your preferences and improve recommendations over time
  • To manage your subscription and process payments via Apple's App Store
  • To send service-related communications (not marketing without your consent)
  • To display advertisements to free-tier users via Google AdMob
  • To check and review comments, handle reports, and stop accounts that break our community rules from commenting

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following trusted third parties to operate our service:

  • Supabase — Cloud database and authentication provider. Stores your account and profile data securely. Data may be hosted in the United States or European Union.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — Processes AI requests: recipe generation, ingredient identification from photos, and reading recipes from photos. Ingredient text, the preferences listed above and images are sent to Anthropic's servers for processing. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • Apple Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — Advertising network used for free-tier users. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve ads. You will be asked for permission before personalized ads are shown (App Tracking Transparency). Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — Handles all subscription payments. We do not store your payment card information.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and security provider for this website.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Meal history is automatically pruned to 90 days. Week history is capped at 12 weeks. You may delete your account at any time, which permanently removes your associated data from our systems (see Section 8). The one exception is a private moderation record of comments: for each comment we keep its text, the email address and account ID of the account that wrote it, and our decision, and for each stopped account its email address and account ID. We keep this even after the account or the comment is deleted, for as long as we need it to enforce our rules and protect other users. It is never shown to other users and is not used for advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

DinnerPilot is designed for use by adults and families, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights — GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at dave@clubhouseapps.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Your Rights — CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at dave@clubhouseapps.com.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your DinnerPilot account and all associated data at any time from within the app: open the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete Account, and type RESET to confirm. Deletion permanently removes your profile, meal history, saved meals, comments, ratings and all other data from our servers, except the private moderation record described in Section 4. This action cannot be undone.

You may also request deletion by emailing dave@clubhouseapps.com. We will process your request within 30 days. For more information, see our Data Deletion Policy.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it on your iPhone in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

9. Cookies and Tracking (Website)

The ClubhouseApps.com website uses no advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Cloudflare, our CDN provider, may set cookies for security and performance purposes. Our email capture forms use Formspree, which processes submitted email addresses according to Formspree's privacy policy.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), row-level security on our database, and JWT-based authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you within the app. Your continued use of DinnerPilot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
