6. How We Use AI, and Its Limits

The recipe catalog was drafted with AI. The recipes in the DinnerPilot catalog were drafted using generative AI. We then checked the catalog with automated reviews (further AI review passes and rule-based checks of ingredients, steps, cooking temperatures and dietary labels) and corrected problems those checks found. Not every problem will have been found or fixed. Some recipes have been cooked and tested, but not all of them, and they have not been reviewed by a dietitian, nutritionist or professional chef. They may contain errors.

Dietary labels are automated. Labels such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free and keto were assigned when the recipes were drafted with AI and were then checked by automated rules against each recipe's ingredient list, assuming a typical version of each ingredient. Products and brands vary, and the labels can be wrong. Always read product labels yourself.

Grocery data is estimated. Matching ingredients to store items, package sizes, ingredient swap suggestions and prices are produced by automated processes, some of which use AI. Prices are estimates and will differ by store and region.

Weekly meal plans are assembled by fixed rules. Your weekly plan is put together on your device from the recipe catalog, using your preferences and a fixed set of rules. No AI model is run when you create or change a plan.

AI features you can use (Pro). Pantry Chef uses AI to identify ingredients in photos you provide and to generate new recipes from the ingredients you give it. Recipe photo scan uses AI to read a photo of a recipe and fill in the new-recipe form for you to check and edit. These results are generated when you ask for them. Nobody reviews them before you see them, and they may be wrong. We pass your dietary restrictions and the foods you avoid to the AI as instructions, and we remove Pantry Chef recipes that name a food on your avoid list, but AI-generated recipes are not otherwise checked against your dietary restrictions. Our AI provider is Anthropic.

AI-generated content, including the recipe catalog, is provided for information and convenience only. We do not guarantee its accuracy, nutritional completeness, or suitability for any dietary or medical need.

The AI features (Pantry Chef recipe generation, pantry photo scanning and recipe photo scan) require a paid Pro subscription, are not available during a free trial, and are subject to daily and monthly limits. When you reach a limit, the app will tell you, and the feature will be available again when the limit resets. We reserve the right to adjust these limits with reasonable notice.

Do not rely solely on DinnerPilot for dietary decisions if you have medical conditions, severe allergies, or other health-related dietary requirements. Always consult a qualified professional for medical dietary advice.
