Questions

Email the studio

When can I get DinnerPilot?

It is in TestFlight for iPhone now; the App Store launch comes next. There is no Android version.

What is the price of Pro?

$5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7‑day free trial. Pantry Chef and photo scan are Pro features with daily limits.

Can I add my own recipes to the plan?

Yes, by hand, or photograph a cookbook page and the app fills the recipe in. The photo scan is a Pro feature.

Are the grocery prices real?

They are estimates from national averages, labeled that way in the app.

What about my family’s privacy?

It is never sold. The privacy policy lists exactly what the app stores.
