Sparky’s Electric

Fictional electrician · Portland, Maine · Est. 2004

Brutal industrial-blueprint 5-page site built as the public-facing portfolio piece for the Studio page. Bungee display + DM Sans body, pitch-black + high-voltage yellow + hazard orange, animated SVG schematic lines drawing across the hero, scrolling voltage-strip marquee, hazard-stripe section dividers, hard-shadow industrial slab buttons, electric-arc cursor effect on CTA hover, rotated vertical brand lettering. Rebuilt as v2 after reading the frontend-design skill.

Scott Fagan Electrical Co.

Family-run residential electrician · Cape Neddick, Maine · Est. 2005

Coastal Craft Editorial. The intentional inverse of Sparky’s: same trade, opposite register. Warm paper-cream + Atlantic ink + copper signage accent. Fraunces variable serif with the SOFT and WONK axes dialed in for editorial warmth, paired with Instrument Sans. Hairline-rule sections, numbered serif markers, italic ampersand drop, and a dark “storm-ready” midsection for whole-home generators. Real 20-year business with no current web presence, built sample-first, pitch-ready.
