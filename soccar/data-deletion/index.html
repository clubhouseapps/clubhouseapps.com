Delete your data

Last updated: November 26, 2026

You can delete your SoccAR account, and the personal data that goes with it, at any time.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Teams you created, and their drill history, cannot be recovered afterwards.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and profile (name and email address)
  • Every team you created, with its drill history and invite codes. Coaches you invited lose access to those teams.
  • Your place on other coaches’ teams
  • The teams and history stored on your iPhone, when you delete from within the app

Drill entries you logged on a team another coach created stay in that team’s history, because they are part of the team’s record, but they are no longer linked to your account.

Deletion from within the app happens immediately. Requests by email are processed within 30 days. Backups containing your data are purged within 90 days.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open SoccAR on your iPhone and sign in
  2. Tap the team name at the top of the screen, then Account
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Option 2 — Ask us by email

Email support@clubhouseapps.com with the subject line "SoccAR Account Deletion" from the address on your account. We will confirm when deletion is complete.

Questions

If you have questions about deleting your data, contact support@clubhouseapps.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
