SoccAR

Your soccer team, organized

SoccAR keeps a youth soccer team organized: the schedule, who’s coming, team messages and the league table, in one app for coaches, the team manager, parents and players. Coaches also get augmented-reality drill layouts on the real field.

For the whole team

  • One schedule. Practices, games and team events with times, fields and directions. Families can add it to the calendar on their phone, and it updates by itself when something changes.
  • Changes reach everyone. When a coach moves a practice, changes a field or cancels a game, parents and players get a notification right away.
  • Who’s coming. Parents mark whether their child is going, can’t go, or might, with a note for the coach. Coaches see the count for every practice and game.
  • News and announcements. Coaches post to parents, players or both, with photos if they like. Parents can heart and comment, and everyone gets a push notification.
  • Team chat. Coaches and parents talk in one team chat (photos welcome); coaches have their own. Before a game, the two teams’ coaches can message each other.
  • Carpools and comments. Offer a ride or book a seat for your child, and ask a question right on a practice or game.
  • Game day. Coaches share the lineup and keep a live score with goals, cards and subs, so families at home can follow along.
  • Every team in one place. Kids on more than one team? See every schedule together, and tap a notification to land on exactly what it’s about. Answer “Going” or “Can’t go” right from the notification.
  • Leagues and standings. Start a league or join one with a code, or import your league’s calendar link. Final scores build the table automatically.

For coaches

  • Drills on the real field. Pick one of more than 30 layouts, aim the camera at the grass and tap. The cones appear life-size where you aimed, with distances in yards.
  • Season plan. Attach drills and your own exercises to each practice, then run the plan on the field drill by drill.
  • Drill history and co-coaches. See what each team ran and when. Invite assistant coaches, and the head coach can name a team manager who can also run the schedule.

Built for kids’ teams

Photos are always optional and private to the team they’re posted to: nobody outside the team can see them. SoccAR never asks for birthdays or players’ contact details; the roster is names and jersey numbers, with an optional photo. Players can see the schedule, news and standings but cannot message anyone. Players under 13 don’t get their own account; their parents see everything they need.

How to get it

SoccAR is in a private beta on TestFlight for iPhone. The drill camera needs an iPhone that supports ARKit (an iPhone XS or later). Families on Android can subscribe to the team calendar in Google Calendar. To ask about the beta, email support@clubhouseapps.com.
