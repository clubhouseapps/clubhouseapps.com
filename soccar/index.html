SoccAR

Lay out drills on the real field

SoccAR lets a soccer coach lay out a drill’s cones on the real field through an iPhone camera, before a single cone comes out of the bag.

Pick a drill from the library, aim at the grass, and tap once. The whole layout appears where you aimed, life-size, with the distance between cones marked in yards. Turn it, slide it, then walk the field and put a real cone on each virtual one.

What it does

  • A drill library. More than 30 layouts across warm-ups, dribbling, passing, possession, shooting, defending, agility and small-sided games, with standard field and goal sizes for 3v3 up to 9v9.
  • One tap to place. The aiming ring turns green when the ground is found. One tap places the drill; tap Clear to start again.
  • Find your cones. A tall center pin marks the middle of the drill, and an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way when it is out of view.
  • Turn and move. Rotate the layout in 15° or 90° steps, or drag it into place.
  • Teams and history. Keep a separate list for each team you coach and see which drills each team ran on which day.
  • Tabletop mode. Shrink any drill onto a table to walk your players through it.

Coaching with others

Coach accounts let you sign in with email, Apple or Google and invite assistant coaches to a team with a code, so everyone sees the same team and the same drill history.

How to get it

SoccAR is in a private beta on TestFlight for iPhone. It needs an iPhone that supports ARKit (an iPhone XS or later). To ask about the beta, email support@clubhouseapps.com.
