Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Clubhouse Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SoccAR iOS application and the ClubhouseApps.com website. This Privacy Policy explains what information SoccAR collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using SoccAR, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create a coach account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your name (if you choose to share it). If you sign up with an email address and password, your password is stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see it.

Teams and coaches: We store the names of the teams you create, which coaches belong to each team and their role (owner or coach), and the invite codes you create to add other coaches, including when each code expires and which account used it.

Drill history: When you place a drill for a team, we record which drill it was, its category, the day, and which coach logged it. The other coaches on that team can see this history.

Camera: SoccAR uses your iPhone’s camera and motion sensors to find the ground and show cones on it. This is processed on your iPhone by Apple’s ARKit. We do not record, store or upload camera images or video, and the app does not take photos.

Information kept on your iPhone: Your teams and drill history are also stored on your iPhone so the app works on the field without a signal. Your chosen drill, cone color and other settings stay on your iPhone.

Device and usage information: SoccAR contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no crash-reporting tools. We do not collect location data. If you install SoccAR through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple’s terms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To keep your teams and drill history in sync across your devices and with the coaches you invite
  • To send service emails, such as confirming your email address and resetting your password (never marketing without your consent)
  • To respond to you when you contact us for support

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties to operate SoccAR:

  • Supabase — Cloud database and authentication provider. Stores your account, teams and drill history. Hosted in the United States.
  • Resend — Sends account emails (email confirmation and password reset) to your address.
  • Apple Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple TestFlight — Distributes the beta app. Governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages — Serve this website.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

Coaches on a team you belong to can see the team’s name, the drill history for that team, and the names of the other coaches on it. Nobody else can see your teams. Anyone with a valid invite code you created can join that team until the code is used or expires, so share codes only with coaches you trust.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time (see Section 9). Unused invite codes expire after 7 days.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

SoccAR is made for adult coaches and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect information about the players on your teams, and you should not enter players’ personal information as team names. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights — GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@clubhouseapps.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Your Rights — CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@clubhouseapps.com.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your SoccAR account from within the app, or by emailing support@clubhouseapps.com. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, the teams you created (with their drill history and invite codes) and your place on other coaches’ teams. Drill entries you logged on another coach’s team stay with that team but are no longer linked to you. For details, see our Data Deletion page.

10. Cookies and Tracking (Website)

The SoccAR pages on ClubhouseApps.com use no advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Cloudflare may set cookies for security and performance purposes. The email confirmation and account pages contact Supabase only when you tap a button on them.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (TLS), row-level security on our database so each coach can reach only their own teams, and token-based authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will notify you within the app. Your continued use of SoccAR after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
