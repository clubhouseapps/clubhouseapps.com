Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Clubhouse Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SoccAR iOS application and the ClubhouseApps.com website. This Privacy Policy explains what information SoccAR collects, how we use it, who can see it, and your rights regarding your data. SoccAR is used by coaches, team managers, parents and players of youth soccer teams, so this policy also explains how we handle information about children.

By using SoccAR, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your name (if you choose to share it). If you sign up with an email address and password, your password is stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see it.

Teams and roles: We store the names of teams, who belongs to each team and their role (head coach, assistant coach, team manager, parent or player), and the invite codes used to join, including when each code expires and which account used it.

Team roster: Coaches and the team manager may enter each player’s first name, last name or initial, and jersey number, and we record which parent accounts are linked to which player. A player’s photo may be added by the team’s staff, the player’s parent, or the player; it is optional. SoccAR does not ask for, and we ask you not to enter, players’ birth dates, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses.

Photos: Coaches may add photos to news posts, coaches and parents may share photos in team chat, and a player’s photo may be added to the roster. Photos are always optional. They are stored privately and only members of the team they were posted to can see them (chat photos: only the people in that chat). Before upload, SoccAR shrinks each photo and removes its hidden details, such as where it was taken.

Schedule and availability: Practices, games and team events (times, places, addresses and notes), saved fields, game scores, lineups, goals, cards and substitutions entered during games (with the player’s first name for our own team), and the answers parents and players give about whether a player is coming, with any note they add. If a coach imports a league calendar link, we store the link and copy the games and events in it onto that team’s schedule.

Carpools and comments: Rides parents offer (direction, seats and a note) and which children are riding, and comments on news posts, practices and games, with hearts on posts.

Contact details you choose to share: If you switch on sharing, the phone number and email address you enter for a team. Nothing is shared unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

Team email: When a coach or the team manager emails the team, we keep a copy of the message and how many people it went to.

Leagues: League names, the teams in each league, league codes, and game results used to build standings.

Messages: Announcements, team chat and coaches’ chat messages (including any photos), and the time each was sent. If you report a message, post, comment or photo, we store the report and the reason. If you block someone, we store that so their messages stay hidden from you.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, we store your iPhone’s push notification token so we can send you team updates, a list of the notifications we sent you and whether you have read them, and the notification settings you choose for each team.

Drill plans and history: The drills and exercises coaches plan for practices, and which drills a team ran on which day.

Camera and photo library: SoccAR uses your iPhone’s camera and motion sensors in the coaches’ drill screen, to find the ground and show cones on it. This is processed on your iPhone by Apple’s ARKit; we do not record or upload those camera images. When you choose to post a photo, you pick it from your photo library and only that photo is uploaded. When you tap Save to Photos, SoccAR adds that photo to your library.

Information kept on your iPhone: A copy of your teams, schedule, roster, announcements and drill history is stored on your iPhone so the app works without a signal. Your chosen drill, cone color and other settings stay on your iPhone.

Device and usage information: SoccAR contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no crash-reporting tools. We do not collect location data; addresses you see are ones a coach typed in. If you install SoccAR through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple’s terms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To show each team’s schedule, roster, messages and standings to the people on that team
  • To send push notifications about news, schedule changes, comments, lineups, goals and game results, and messages
  • To send team emails that a coach or the team manager writes
  • To import a league calendar that a coach links
  • To provide a private calendar feed of the team schedule
  • To review reported messages and enforce our Terms
  • To send service emails, such as confirming your email address and resetting your password (never marketing without your consent)
  • To respond to you when you contact us for support

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties to operate SoccAR:

  • Supabase — Cloud database, private photo storage, authentication and server functions. Stores the information described above. Hosted in the United States.
  • Apple Push Notification service — Delivers notifications to your iPhone. Apple receives your device token and the text of each notification.
  • Resend — Sends account emails (email confirmation and password reset) and team emails written by your team’s coaches or manager.
  • Apple Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple TestFlight — Distributes the beta app. Governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages — Serve this website.

If you subscribe to a team calendar, the calendar app you use (for example Apple Calendar or Google Calendar) fetches the schedule from us and handles it under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

  • Coaches and the team manager see everything about their team: the roster and player photos, which parents are linked to which player, the schedule, everyone’s availability answers, news, comments, carpools, the team chat and coaches’ chat, lineups, drill plans and drill history.
  • Parents see the roster and player photos, the schedule, their own children’s availability answers (and everyone’s, if the coaches turn that on), news sent to parents, comments, carpools, published lineups, live scores, the team chat, shared contact details and standings. They cannot see drill plans or the coaches’ chat.
  • Players see the schedule, the roster and player photos, news sent to players, published lineups, live scores and standings. They cannot see or send messages, comments or contact details.
  • Another team’s coaches and families see a game you are playing against them (time, place, score, and when goals, cards and subs happened, but not your players’ names), and the opposing coaches see that game’s coaches-only chat. They see nothing else about your team, including its photos.
  • League members see the league’s team names, its games and its standings.
  • Anyone with your team’s calendar link can see the team schedule (times, places and notes, but no names of players). Share it only within the team.

Your name and email address are visible to the other members of your teams. Anyone with a valid invite code can join a team until the code is used or expires, so share codes only with the person they are meant for.

5. Data Retention

We keep team information for as long as the team exists, and your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time (see Section 9). Unused invite codes expire after 7 days.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

SoccAR is used to organize children’s sports teams, so we limit what we collect about children:

  • Children under 13 may not create an account. Information about a player under 13 is limited to the first name, last name or initial, and jersey number a coach enters on the roster, and the attendance answers their parent gives. We use it only to run the team, and only that team’s members can see it.
  • Players aged 13 to 17 may be invited by a coach to a view-only account. These accounts cannot send messages, and we collect only the account information described in Section 1.
  • We never ask for players’ birth dates or contact details. Photos are optional and only visible to the player’s own team. A parent may remove their child’s player photo in the app at any time, or ask the coach or us to remove any photo of their child.

A parent may ask to see, correct or delete information about their child at any time by asking the team’s coach or by emailing support@clubhouseapps.com. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without a parent’s knowledge, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights — GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@clubhouseapps.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Your Rights — CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@clubhouseapps.com.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your SoccAR account from within the app, or by emailing support@clubhouseapps.com. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, the teams you created (with their roster, schedule, messages and history), the leagues you organize, your place on other teams, your shared contact details, your notification tokens and settings, and your blocks and reports. Photos stored for teams you started are deleted too. Messages, photos, comments, announcements and drill entries you posted on teams you did not create stay with those teams, because they are part of the team’s record, but they are no longer linked to your name. You can delete your own messages, comments and photos in the app before deleting your account. For details, see our Data Deletion page.

10. Cookies and Tracking (Website)

The SoccAR pages on ClubhouseApps.com use no advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Cloudflare may set cookies for security and performance purposes. The email confirmation, account and join pages contact Supabase only when you tap a button on them. When a coach imports a calendar link, our server fetches that link; the site it comes from sees a request from our server, not from you.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (TLS), row-level security on our database so each person can reach only the teams they belong to and only what their role allows, and token-based authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will notify you within the app. Your continued use of SoccAR after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
