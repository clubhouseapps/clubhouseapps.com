1. Description of Service

SoccAR is an iOS application that uses augmented reality to show soccer drill layouts on a real field, so a coach can see where to place cones. It includes a library of drill layouts, team lists, drill history, and shared teams for coaches who work together. SoccAR is currently offered free of charge as a beta through Apple’s TestFlight.
