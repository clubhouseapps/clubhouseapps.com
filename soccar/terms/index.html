Terms of service

Last updated: November 27, 2026

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your use of SoccAR, provided by Clubhouse Apps ("we," "our," or "us"). By downloading or using SoccAR, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

1. Description of Service

SoccAR is an iOS application for organizing youth soccer teams. It includes a team schedule with a calendar feed and calendar import, attendance, carpools, news with optional photos, comments, team email, announcements and push notifications, team and coaches’ chat, lineups and live scoring, leagues and standings, and, for coaches, practice planning, drill history and augmented-reality drill layouts on a real field. SoccAR is currently offered free of charge as a beta through Apple’s TestFlight.

2. Beta Software

SoccAR is pre-release software. Features may change, stop working, or be removed, and data may be lost. Notifications may arrive late or not at all. Do not rely on SoccAR as the only way to tell families about urgent changes, such as a game cancelled for weather or a safety issue. Beta builds expire on the schedule Apple sets for TestFlight.

3. Eligibility and Roles

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live, to create a coach, team manager or parent account, or to start a team or league.
  • Players aged 13 to 17 may use a view-only player account if a coach invites them, with their parent’s permission. Children under 13 may not create an account.
  • The coach who starts a team is its head coach. The head coach decides who is an assistant coach, team manager or parent, and can remove anyone from the team.

4. Accounts, Teams and Invite Codes

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details private and for all activity under your account. Notify us at support@clubhouseapps.com if you suspect unauthorized access.

Coaches and team managers are responsible for who they invite. Give each invite code only to the person it is for; a code links a parent to a specific player. Keep the team calendar link within the team.

If you add players to a roster, you confirm that you are entitled to do so for your team (for example, as its coach or manager) and you will enter only names or initials and jersey numbers, not birth dates or contact details.

Photos. Photos are optional. Post only photos you have the right to share and that the families of any children shown are comfortable with; remove a photo of a child if their parent asks. Never post photos that show a child undressed, in a changing area, in distress, or with personal details such as a school name or home address. We may remove any photo and suspend accounts that misuse photos.

Calendar import. Import only a calendar link you are allowed to use for your own team, such as one your league or club gives you. You are responsible for following that site’s terms.

Contact details and email. Contact details others share with the team are for team matters only. Team email is for team business; don’t use it for advertising or anything unrelated to the team.

5. Messages and Conduct

Announcements and chats are for running the team. When you post, you agree to:

  • Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual content, or content that is illegal or harmful.
  • Never post anything that sexualizes, endangers or exploits a child, or that shares a child’s personal information beyond what the team needs.
  • Not use SoccAR to contact players privately. SoccAR does not allow messages to or from players.
  • Not post spam, advertising, or other people’s private information.
  • Not post photos of other families’ children against their wishes.

You can report any message, post, comment or photo from inside the app and block anyone’s messages. We review reports, may remove messages that break these Terms, and may suspend or remove accounts that do. Where the law requires it, or where a child may be at risk, we may report content to the authorities.

6. Safety on the Field

Watch where you are going. Do not walk or run while looking at your screen near players, balls, goals, traffic, water or other hazards. Stay aware of your surroundings at all times.

Placement is approximate. Augmented reality tracking can drift, especially over long distances, on plain or uneven ground, or in poor light. Cone positions and distances shown in the app are estimates. Check measurements yourself when they matter.

Drills are for guidance only. The drill layouts are general examples. You are responsible for choosing activities that are safe and suitable for your players’ ages, abilities and conditions, and for following the rules of your club, league and governing body.

Schedules and standings come from users. Times, places and scores are entered by coaches, team managers and league organizers. We do not check them. Standings in SoccAR are not official unless your league says they are.
